# AI Boss Finance

Your AI Finance Assistant from The AI Boss Society. It keeps your money organized, prepares a real profit and loss statement every month, and gets you tax-ready, without ever touching your money.

| AI Employee | Start setup by saying | Setup time | Then use it by saying |
|---|---|---|---|
| business-ai-finance-assistant | "Set up my finance assistant" | about 25 min | "Money check," "Prepare my P&L for [month]," "Who owes me?", "Get my accountant packet ready," "Get me ready for taxes" |

## What it does
- **Weekly money check:** new transactions sorted into your categories, plus alerts, unpaid invoices, and anything that needs your eyes.
- **Monthly, quarterly, and annual profit and loss statements:** spreadsheets with real formulas, compared to year-to-date and last year.
- **Who owes me:** your unpaid invoices, with reminder emails drafted for you to review.
- **Estimated-tax reminders:** before each due date. Reminders only; your accountant sets the amounts.
- **Accountant packet and year-end checklist:** everything your accountant needs, organized by quarter and year.

## How to start
Start a Cowork task and say **"Set up my finance assistant."** It will first ask whether your Business Brain folder is attached, then walk you through 12 questions, one at a time. Already have notes from your accountant or a list of categories? Upload them, and it only asks about what's missing. Stop anytime; say the same phrase later to pick up where you left off.

No accounting software? No problem. Each month, download your business bank and card statements as CSV files and drop them in a `finance-uploads` folder inside your Business Brain folder.

## Built-in safety
It never pays, moves, or requests money. It never files taxes, stores full account numbers, or deletes anything, and it never gives tax advice. Questions like "Can I deduct this?" go on a list for your accountant. Every report notes that it was prepared from your records and not reviewed by a CPA.
